How range() Works
Learn it, then crush 4 games! 🎮
for i in range(5):← runs 5 times!
    print("🐠 Wave #", i)← i = 0,1,2,3,4
🔑 for starts loop 🔢 i = counter 📡 range(n) = n times ➡️ indented = repeats
range(5)0 up to not including 5
01234
range(2, 6)Start 2, stop before 6
2345
range(0, 10, 2)Step 2 — even numbers!
02468
range(3, 0, -1)Step -1 — backwards!
321
🤿 Key rule: range(n) starts at 0 and gives n numbers. range(5) = 0,1,2,3,4. The stop number is never included!
