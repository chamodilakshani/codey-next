Loop with print()
Learn the 4 rules, then play! 🎮
for i in range(4): ← loops 4 times
    print("🫧 Bubble!") ← runs 4 times!
↪️
Always indent 4 spaces print() must be inside the loop
    print("hi")
🔤
Text needs "quotes" single or double both work
print("hello 🌊")
🔢
i = no quotes needed it's a number, not text
print("Wave", i)
✨
f-strings mix text + i put f before quote, use {i}
print(f"Fish {i}!")
🐠 range(1,4) + print(f"Fish {i} swims!") →  Fish 1 swims!   Fish 2 swims!   Fish 3 swims!
